## My Favorite Movies & Series

Welcome to my repository! Here is a breakdown of my favorite cinematic experiences using various Markdown elements.

### My Absolute Favorite: **Interstellar**

*Interstellar* is a 2014 epic science fiction film directed by Christopher Nolan. It is my all-time favorite movie because it perfectly balances mind-bending physics with raw human emotion.

> "Mankind was born on Earth. It was never meant to die here."

#### What Makes It Great
- **The Score:** Hans Zimmer's pipe organ soundtrack is legendary.
- **The Visuals:** The depiction of the black hole, Gargantua, is stunningly accurate.
- **The Story:** A heartbreaking look at time, love, and survival.
